### Aaron Stalberg

I build AI tools and AI-powered businesses. Based in the United Kingdom.

Most of what I build sits at the point where AI systems decide what to show people — the answers
ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity and Gemini give when someone asks who or what to trust.

**What I'm building**

- **[ShowUp Labs](https://www.showuplabs.com)** — AI search visibility. Measures whether AI assistants
  recommend a business, shows which competitors they name instead, and what to change.
- **[AutoCoreAgent](https://www.autocoreagent.com)** — AI automation and LinkedIn tooling.

**What I write about**

AI search visibility, answer engine optimisation (AEO), generative engine optimisation (GEO), large
language model visibility, and the operational side of running AI automation in production.
